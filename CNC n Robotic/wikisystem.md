# 🧭 Hermes ↔ Obsidian Wiki System

## Pattern Overview

This folder stores knowledge **ingested from**, or created **by** [Hermès](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com) sessions during our work together. Notes use [[wikilink]]s for cross-references and maintain YAML frontmatter tags for retrieval.

---

## Folders you may want

**📌 Core patterns:**
- `/patterns/` - Standard note templates & best practices
- `/ingested-from-hermes/` - Notes we created together from URLs or research
- `/memories/` - Personal facts about your tools, workflows, preferences

**🔍 Optional utilities:**
- `/search/` - Index notes with `wiki_ingest.py` for fast queries
- `/backlinks/` - Auto-generated backlink maps (scripted)
