## Evolution Timeline

### Era 1: Industrial Giants (1950s-1980s)
- **Unimate** (1961) – First industrial robot; programmed by tape
- **PUMA** (1979) – Palletizing robot; articulated arm precursor  
- **Features**: Bulky, dedicated tasks, no intelligence

### Era 2: Programmable Flexibility (1980s-1990s)  
- ** teach-by-showing** robots emerge
- **Joint control** → Cartesian coordination
- CAD integration begins

### Era 3: Vision & Perception (1990s-2000s)
- Camera guidance, force sensing
- Collision detection added
- Multi-axis coordination

### Era 4: Collaborative Age (2010s-Present) ← We're here! 🎯
- **Cobots**: Safe human collaboration
- **AI integration**: ML perception layers
- **Edge computing**: On-board decision making
- **Swarm coordination**: Multi-robot systems
