   - Material used (e.g., "baltic birch plywood 3/8")
   - Bits used (end mill 6mm, 2-flute carbide)
   - G-code snippet you exported
   - Results (cut quality, tool wear, issues encountered)

   Add vendor URLs for tooling suppliers or free tutorials from Fusion 360 CAM docs.
