## Recommendations

1. **Professional Miners:** RTX 4090s with cheap electricity (<$0.10/kWh) and low-fee pools can generate $2,000-$5,000+ annually per GPU
2. **Home Users:** Avoid mining PEARL on single gaming PCs due to hardware wear, electricity costs, and price volatility
3. **Alternative Strategies:**
   - Rent out GPU hardware instead of mining
   - Cloud mining arbitrage opportunities
   - Consider alternative coins with higher stability

## Risk Factors

- **Price Volatility:** PEARL dropped 41% from $1.00 to $0.59
- **Network Competition:** 6 pools now competing for rewards (was 2)
- **Pool Fees:** High-fee pools (5%) can reduce profits by 20-25%
- **Hardware Degradation:** 24/7 mining shortens GPU lifespan
- **Warranty Void:** Mining typically voids manufacturer warranties
